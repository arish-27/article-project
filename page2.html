How IT Jobs Are Changing / Chapter 02

Expected Changes in
IT Employment by 2030.

As companies adopt more AI tools, the daily routine of software engineers is shifting. Instead of spending hours typing standard repetitive code, engineers will spend more time designing big systems and ensuring software works safely and reliably.

Why Human Review Matters

“AI tools can write code fast, but they still make mistakes. Human engineers are needed to double-check the work, design how everything connects, and take responsibility when real money or safety is on the line.”

Why Humans Matter

Why Human Engineers Will Always Be Needed

Computers cannot take legal responsibility, negotiate with business clients, or understand human emotions and real-world common sense. When critical systems go down, human judgment and problem-solving are what save the day.

1

Understanding Real-World Client Needs

Turning vague client ideas into clear, workable software plans that actually solve business problems.

2

Fixing Live Outages & Taking Responsibility

Finding and fixing the true root cause when websites or apps crash under heavy traffic.

3

Privacy, Security & Legal Compliance

Making sure software respects user privacy, protects personal data, and follows government laws.

New Paths for College Graduates

How Freshers and Students Can Succeed

In the past, large IT companies hired tens of thousands of college graduates each year and trained them for months on basic manual tasks. Today, companies look for graduates who already know how to use AI tools to solve problems and build real projects.

The Old Way (2020–2024)
Typing standard code manually, basic software maintenance, and simple bug fixes.
The New Way (2026–2030)
Understanding client needs, guiding AI assistants to write code, checking outputs for mistakes, and making sure the final app works reliably.

Executive Summary Matrix

Key Findings of the IT 2030 Report:

  • Routine Tasks Handled by AI: Up to 80% of basic repetitive coding, standard testing, and template scripts could be automated by AI tools by 2028.
  • Higher Pay for System Designers: Senior engineers who know how to design large, safe, and reliable systems can earn up to 4.2x more than standard coders.
  • Local Business Impact: Every direct IT job supports 3.8 local service livelihoods (such as rental housing, food stalls, transport, and stores). Changes in IT hiring affect PG housing (-22%), cafeterias, and local buses.
  • Chennai's Strong Foundation: With over 35% of India's car exports, world-class hospitals, deep shipping ports, and software product companies, Chennai is well-positioned for the future.
