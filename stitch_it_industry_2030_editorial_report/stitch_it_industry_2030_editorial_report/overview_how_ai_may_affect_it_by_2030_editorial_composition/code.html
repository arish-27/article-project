How AI May Affect the IT Industry by 2030

Artificial intelligence will not destroy the IT industry—it will structurally invert it. The past 30 years of human syntax assembly, offshore maintenance arbitrage, and linear headcount scaling are collapsing into autonomous synthesis and high-leverage architectural orchestration.

The Three-Epoch Trajectory (2024 – 2030)

Structural transformation in industrial software does not occur instantaneously; it advances through three distinct infrastructural stages.

2024 – 2026
Epoch 01

The Copilot & Syntax Phase

Assisted coding reaches universal saturation in modern IDEs. Productivity spikes 20-35% in boilerplate generation, but fundamental software architecture remains human-led and human-approved.

IDE Copilot saturation crosses 92% Immediate hiring freeze on entry-level offshore coders

Autonomous Agent Swarms

Autonomous multi-agent clusters formulate pull requests, run regression suites, and execute deployments without manual code touches. The billing model transitions irrevocably from contract hours to compute consumption.
