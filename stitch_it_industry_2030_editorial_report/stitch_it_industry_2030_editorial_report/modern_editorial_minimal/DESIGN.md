## Brand & Style
This design system embodies an ultra-refined, progressive editorial aesthetic rooted in contemporary Scandinavian and Swiss architectural clarity. It pairs serene, spacious, light neutral backgrounds with razor-sharp dark geometric typography, punctuated by calculated accents of warm coral terracotta and deep obsidian slate.

The visual language communicates intelligence, technical mastery, and effortless luxury. Designed for high-end AI research labs, next-generation deep tech SaaS, modern architectural practices, and forward-looking digital ventures, it prioritizes generous breathing room, structured asymmetric bento layouts, floating pill controls, and subtle translucent layering over chaotic visual clutter. The interface creates an atmosphere of intellectual clarity and modern craftsmanship.

## Colors
The palette balances light airy canvas zones with deep carbon-black anchor surfaces.

- **Primary (`#111215`)**: Obsidian black used for high-impact typography, dark accent cards, inverted feature hero blocks, and active pill indicators.
- **Secondary (`#e05244`)**: Terracotta crimson coral. Deployed strategically as an energetic focal point: gradient text accents, highlighted keyword spans, high-contrast banner tapes, and interactive hover feedback.
- **Tertiary (`#6366f1`)**: Indigo iris tint. Serves as a subtle gradient companion to the coral accent in iridescent mesh headers, futuristic graphics, and subtle technical badges.
- **Neutral (`#f8f9fa`)**: Clean off-white/sand canvas base. Paired with pure white (`#ffffff`) for elevated foreground cards and crisp hairline dividers (`#e5e7eb`). Dark container surfaces shift to `#141416` and `#1c1c1e` for dramatic inverted contrast sections.

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans provides geometric balance with contemporary warmth and razor-sharp clarity.

Headlines employ tight negative letter-spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.035em`) to capture an editorial publication aesthetic. Key headline words leverage subtle dual-tone color transitions (mixing dark obsidian with gradient coral-indigo spans). Body copy maintains comfortable leading (`1.5` to `1.6`) for rapid scanning across complex technical data. Section super-titles utilize slash prefixing (e.g., `/ services /`, `/ about /`) rendered in muted uppercase tracking.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a 12-column asymmetric grid with generous canvas gutters and deep vertical breathing room between narrative milestones.

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column grid with `2rem` gutters and outer margins scaling up to `5rem`. Bento sections combine variable-width spans (4-col + 8-col, 5-col + 7-col) for dynamic asymmetrical cadence.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters and `2.5rem` margins. Bento cards condense to balanced 2-column arrays.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column single flow, `1rem` gutters, and `1.25rem` margins. Horizontal galleries and bento grids convert cleanly into vertical stacks.
- **Vertical Spacing**: Section paddings utilize `space-2xl` (`4rem`) to `space-3xl` (`6rem`), guaranteeing clear content demarcation without heavy horizontal dividing lines.

## Elevation & Depth
This design system rejects heavy, muddy drop shadows in favor of crisp surface-level boundaries and luminous textural depth:

- **Hairline Outlines**: Surface boundaries rely primarily on 1px borders rendered in `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)` on light mode and `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` on dark mode.
- **Translucent Layering & Glassmorphism**: Navigation bars, pill floats, and auxiliary control overlays utilize frosted glass treatments (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)` background fill).
- **Deep Contrast Blocks**: Rather than stacking z-index layers, depth is established via alternating light and deep dark content plates (`#111215` / `#161618`), immediately focusing user attention.
- **Ambient Glows**: Reserved exclusively for media showcase backplates and interactive CTA highlights using diffused secondary tinting (`rgba(224, 82, 68, 0.12)` blur radius 32px-48px).

## Shapes
The shape philosophy merges generous card curvatures with strict capsule pill geometry.

Interactive control elements (buttons, badges, floating search tabs, filter items) consistently use fully rounded pill shapes (`9999px`). Content containers, media wrappers, and bento cards employ exaggerated modern border radii ranging between `1.5rem` (`24px`) and `2rem` (`32px`), giving interfaces an organic, humanistic flow while maintaining structural grid integrity.

## Components

### Buttons & Navigation Pills
- **Primary Pill**: Inverted solid black pill (`#111215`) with crisp white text, padding `0.625rem 1.375rem`, optional small circular trailing icon.
- **Secondary Pill**: Hairline outline (`1px solid #d1d5db`), transparent or pure white fill, dark neutral text. On hover, background transitions smoothly to `#f3f4f6`.
- **Capsule Navigation Bar**: Floating island navigation enclosed in a pill shell with subtle blur backdrop and hairline perimeter. Active item is highlighted using an obsidian black pill chip.

### Bento & Media Cards
- **Light Bento Card**: Pure white background, `1.5rem` corner radius, `1px solid #e5e7eb` boundary, `1.5rem` internal padding. Integrated pill tags sit at the top, followed by strong titles and micro-metric badges.
- **Dark Media Card**: `#141416` dark foundation with nested 3D graphic renders or organic abstract imagery. Textures sit beneath low-opacity dark gradients, featuring circular arrow discovery buttons anchored to the lower-right corner.

### Chips & Section Badges
- Slender pill tags (`padding: 0.25rem 0.75rem`) using uppercase label typography. Super-titles leverage subtle prefix formatting (e.g., `/ services /`) with muted slate hues.

### Accordion & Lists
- Minimalist horizontal rules (`1px solid #e5e7eb`) with fluid vertical spacing (`1.5rem`). Expandable rows feature understated typography with right-aligned hairline plus/arrow triggers.

### Inputs & Forms
- Rounded pill text inputs (`border: 1px solid #e5e7eb`, background: `#ffffff`, padding: `0.75rem 1.25rem`) that transition to a crisp `#111215` outline on focus.
