2026–2030 Outlook — Research Summary

How AI May Affect the IT Industry by 2030

Artificial intelligence will not destroy the IT industry—it is changing how people work. For over 30 years, tech companies grew by hiring large teams to write repetitive code and maintain older systems. Now, AI can write basic code in seconds, shifting human work toward designing systems, solving business problems, and ensuring quality.

Key Findings

By 2028, over 80% of routine, repetitive coding tasks could be written automatically by AI. By 2030, tech companies will increasingly charge clients for completed business results rather than just billing hours worked.

How the Industry Changes (2024 – 2030)

The transition in software development is expected to happen across three stages.

2024 – 2026 Stage 01

The AI Assistant Phase

AI coding assistants become common in everyday work. Developers finish routine tasks 20-35% faster, while human engineers still make all major decisions and approve code.

Most developers use AI assistants daily (92%+)
Hiring slows for purely repetitive, manual coding jobs
2027 – 2028 Stage 02

Autonomous AI Workflows

AI agent teams write features, test them, and deploy updates together. Companies start billing clients for completed business results rather than hours worked.

Routine bug fixes and maintenance handled automatically
Shift away from traditional hourly billing contracts
2029 – 2030 Stage 03

The On-Demand Software Era

Custom software can be created rapidly whenever needed. IT firms win business based on guaranteed business outcomes, data security, and specialized industry expertise.

Outcome-based contracts become the standard
Engineers focus on high-impact problem solving

Computers cannot take legal responsibility, negotiate with business clients, or understand human emotions and real-world common sense. When critical systems go down, human judgment and problem-solving are what save the day.

Paths for College Graduates

How Freshers and Students Can Succeed

In the past, large IT companies hired tens of thousands of college graduates each year and trained them for months on basic manual tasks. Today, companies look for graduates who already know how to use AI tools to solve problems and build real projects.

The Past Approach
Typing standard code manually, basic software maintenance, and simple bug fixes.
The Future Approach
Understanding client needs, guiding AI assistants to write code, checking outputs for mistakes, and making sure the final app works reliably.

Executive Summary: Key Takeaways

Key Points from the 2030 IT Industry Report:

  • Routine Code Automation: Up to 80% of repetitive code, basic testing, and routine scripts can be generated automatically by AI tools by 2028.
