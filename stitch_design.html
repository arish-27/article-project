The Multiplier Effect of Indian IT

According to macroeconomic analyses by NASSCOM and urban research institutes, every formal software engineer in an Indian metro creates an indirect livelihood for approximately 3.5 to 4 individuals in transport, hospitality, residential facilities, and retail. When AI automates the bottom layer of coding, city centers experience indirect commercial shockwaves.
